Biography

I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Computer Science at Peking University, advised by Sheng Li (Professor), Guoping Wang (Professor), and Jiaguang Sun (Member of the Chinese Academy of Engineering). My research focuses on photorealistic rendering and high-performance real-time rendering, with particular interest in practical methods for efficient light transport simulation.

Research Interests

  • Monte Carlo Rendering
  • Path Tracing / Ray Tracing
  • Neural Rendering
